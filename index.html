02

Not another black cylinder.

Every smart speaker looks the same: a plastic puck, a fabric cylinder, a little computer apologizing for existing. People have kept maneki-neko on their shelves for centuries - in shops, in kitchens, by front doors - because a lucky cat is a charm, not an appliance.

So that is where the computer goes. Lucky Cat is a voice assistant you would keep on the shelf even if it never said a word.

03

A small computer with a point of view.

The first prototype runs on a Raspberry Pi 5. Its voice loop uses Gemini while the local model is tested and tuned. The physical Pi has woken to its name; local speech is a next build step. The aim is still a cat that can keep the everyday loop in the room.

  • EarsUSB microphone; local speech next
  • BrainGemini prototype; fine-tuned Qwen 3B in testing
  • VoiceSpeaker output working; open TTS planned
  • Body3.5-inch screen face; moving shell in development
  • PersonasFive packs, hot-swappable, open format
  • SoftwareOne evolving stack, built in public
